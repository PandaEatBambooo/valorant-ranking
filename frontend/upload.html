1. Spreadsheet Format

  • Required columns: Name, ACS, KD, KDA, Win_Rate, Headshot
  • Optional columns: Agent, Matches
  • Column names are not case-sensitive, and spaces/％ signs are fine (e.g. "Win Rate %" also works)
  • Win_Rate and Headshot are plain numbers, e.g. 65 for 65%
  • Max 15 players per account — extra rows will be skipped, not rejected outright
